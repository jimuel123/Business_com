About

Clarity from complexity.

I work at the intersection of business needs and software delivery. My objective is to make sure stakeholders and engineering teams share a clear understanding of the problem, the requirements, and what success looks like.

From discovery to delivery, I help teams turn conversations into structured requirements, useful models, testable acceptance criteria, and aligned outcomes.
